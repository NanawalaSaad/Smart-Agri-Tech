🎯 Our Mission

Saudi Arabia imports over 80% of its food. We are solving this by building an AI system that tells farmers exactly what to grow, when to water, and how to optimize — even in 55°C desert heat.

Our system combines IoT hardware (ESP32 sensors) with machine learning (XGBoost + deep learning) to create a complete farm intelligence platform — aligned with Saudi Vision 2030's food security goals.
