# Research Novelty

## Problem
Desert agriculture suffers from water scarcity, harsh climate and inefficient crop planning.

## Proposed Solution
A hybrid Edge–Cloud IoT framework integrating ESP32 sensors with an Expert System for intelligent crop recommendation.

## Novel Contributions
- Real-time environmental monitoring
- Intelligent crop recommendation
- Automated irrigation scheduling
- Yield estimation
- Cloud dashboard
- Low-cost architecture

## Future Scope

- Weather APIs
- Satellite imagery
